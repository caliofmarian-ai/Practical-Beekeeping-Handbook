# Illustration Plan — Chapter 36

## Chapter

Chapter 36 — Record Keeping

## Purpose

This document defines professional visual material for the final illustration and layout stage. Artwork is not inserted into the manuscript during drafting.

## Proposed Illustrations

### Illustration 36.1 — Complete Apiary Record System

**Type:** Information-flow diagram

**Purpose:** Show the relationship among colony, queen, treatment, feeding, harvest, batch, equipment, sales, and financial records.

**Required content:**

- colony identifier at the centre;
- linked queen record;
- linked health record;
- linked feeding record;
- linked harvest batch;
- apiary-level summary;
- business-level summary.

### Illustration 36.2 — Colony Inspection Record Sheet

**Type:** Annotated professional form

**Required fields:**

- colony identity;
- date and weather;
- inspection purpose;
- strength;
- queen evidence;
- brood;
- stores;
- health;
- actions;
- next trigger.

### Illustration 36.3 — Identification Hierarchy

**Type:** Structured coding graphic

**Purpose:** Demonstrate how apiary, colony, queen, equipment, and batch codes relate.

### Illustration 36.4 — Paper, Digital, and Hybrid Systems

**Type:** Three-column comparison

**Purpose:** Compare advantages, limitations, backup, searchability, and field use.

### Illustration 36.5 — Treatment Traceability Chain

**Type:** Compliance flow diagram

**Required content:**

- monitoring;
- decision;
- authorized product;
- batch and expiry;
- application;
- withdrawal period;
- post-treatment check;
- retained record.

### Illustration 36.6 — Honey Batch Traceability

**Type:** Farm-to-container flow

**Required content:**

- apiary;
- colony or super group;
- harvest date;
- extraction;
- processing;
- container;
- storage;
- sale;
- complaint investigation.

### Illustration 36.7 — Record Review Cycle

**Type:** Circular review graphic

**Stages:**

- field observation;
- entry;
- quality check;
- weekly review;
- monthly comparison;
- seasonal analysis;
- annual decision.

## Tables Recommended for Final Layout

- colony inspection template;
- queen-performance record;
- treatment record;
- feeding record;
- harvest and batch record;
- equipment inventory;
- annual apiary summary.

## Visual Style

- publication quality;
- clear professional forms;
- readable typography;
- consistent coding examples;
- neutral colours;
- realistic apiary terminology;
- no unsafe or misleading practices;
- no implication that one record format satisfies every jurisdiction.

## Final Production Checks

- verify all form fields against the final manuscript;
- confirm identifiers remain consistent;
- confirm treatment examples do not imply unauthorized use;
- confirm traceability chain remains legally neutral;
- coordinate with later food-safety and business chapters;
- confirm numbering during final layout.

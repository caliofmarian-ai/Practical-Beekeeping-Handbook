## Accuracy Requirements

- Adding managed honey-bee hives must not be depicted as a default wild-pollinator conservation measure.
- AIPP must be labelled voluntary, not statutory.
- Pesticide guidance must not override product labels or local law.
- Artificial nesting structures must not be portrayed as a substitute for natural habitat.
- Sensitive-site hive-density figures must avoid a universal numerical threshold.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, patterns and shapes in addition to colour.
- Seasonal and habitat figures must remain legible in greyscale.
- Conservation hierarchy should be readable as text even without icons.

# Chapter 32 — Queen Rearing: Illustration Plan

## Status

Planned for the final illustration and layout stage.

## Purpose

The visuals should explain queen development, larval selection, cell building, mating nuclei and queen evaluation.

## Visual 1 — Queen Development Timeline

Show:

- Egg.
- newly hatched larva.
- open queen cell.
- sealed queen cell.
- emergence.
- maturation.
- mating flights.
- first eggs.

## Visual 2 — Larval Age Selection

Compare:

- Egg.
- ideal newly hatched larva.
- larva too old for grafting.

Include realistic scale and brood-food appearance.

## Visual 3 — Breeder-Colony Selection

Show desirable and undesirable traits:

- Strong brood.
- good temperament.
- health.
- winter survival.
- disease warning signs.
- excessive swarm tendency.

## Visual 4 — Maternal and Paternal Genetics

Show:

- Breeder queen.
- daughter virgin queen.
- multiple drone sources.
- resulting worker subfamilies.

## Visual 5 — Starter and Finisher Colonies

Compare:

- Queenless starter.
- queenright or queenless finisher.
- nurse-bee concentration.
- cell-bar position.

## Visual 6 — Grafting Sequence

Show:

1. Selecting young larva.
2. sliding tool beneath larva.
3. preserving orientation.
4. placing larva in cup.
5. transferring cell bar.

## Visual 7 — Grafting Tools

Show:

- Chinese grafting tool.
- metal needle.
- brush.
- magnifier.
- light.
- damp protective cloth.

## Visual 8 — Accepted and Rejected Cells

Compare:

- Accepted cup with royal jelly and wax extension.
- rejected empty cup.
- damaged larva.
- dried larva.

## Visual 9 — Non-Grafting Methods

Compare:

- Cut-cell method.
- cell punching.
- direct-lay cup system.
- selected brood-frame method.

## Visual 10 — Queen-Cell Protection

Show:

- Correct upright handling.
- insulated transport.

Show fields for:

## Style Direction

- Professional scientific handbook style.
- Accurate brood and queen proportions.
- Clear developmental timing.
- Consistent hive equipment.
- Natural colours.
- No commercial branding.
- No cartoon anthropomorphism.
- Suitable for print and digital publication.

## Production Notes

All visuals should be created during final layout with consistent typography, line weight, captions, dimensions and colour treatment.

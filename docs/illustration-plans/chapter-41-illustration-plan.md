# Chapter 41 — Varroa Destructor: Illustration Plan

Status: planned for final layout. No provisional artwork is to be embedded in the manuscript.

## Planned Figures

### Figure 41.1 — Varroa Life Cycle in Worker Brood
Brood-cell sequence: foundress entry before capping, establishment of feeding site, egg laying, offspring development, mating and emergence of mature daughters.

### Figure 41.2 — Worker Versus Drone Brood
Side-by-side timeline showing longer drone capping and why it can support greater mite reproductive success. Avoid false precision where strain or temperature changes timing.

### Figure 41.3 — Adult-Bee Feeding Site
Detailed worker anatomy with mite position between body segments and inset showing fat-body tissue as a major feeding target.

### Figure 41.4 — Varroa–Virus–Colony Decline Pathway
Causal flow: mite feeding and virus transmission → compromised developing bees → shorter adult lifespan → inadequate replacement → colony decline.

### Figure 41.5 — Seasonal Mite-to-Bee Ratio
Conceptual graph showing bee population and mite population trajectories in a temperate example. Caption must state that timing varies by climate and management.

### Figure 41.6 — Adult-Bee Wash Procedure
Numbered visual: prepare container, choose brood frame, locate/protect queen, collect validated sample, wash/dislodge mites, count mites, calculate mites per 100 bees, record result.

### Figure 41.7 — Monitoring Methods Compared
Adult-bee wash, sugar roll, natural mite fall and brood uncapping, with columns for what each method measures, whether bees are sacrificed, and main limitations.

### Figure 41.8 — Mites per 100 Bees Calculation
Simple worked mathematical example using nine mites in 300 bees = three mites per 100 bees. Separate calculation from region-specific intervention decision.

### Figure 41.9 — Why Thresholds Are Contextual
Decision wheel: season, brood, climate, virus pressure, reinvasion, treatment options, management goal and local guidance.

### Figure 41.10 — Varroa Control Categories
Non-product-specific infographic: resistant stock, drone-brood removal, brood interruption, authorised synthetic acaricides, organic-acid products, other registered products, monitoring and verification.

### Figure 41.11 — Treatment Failure Versus Reinvasion
Timeline with pre-treatment count, treatment, immediate verification and later retest; show how different patterns suggest poor efficacy versus later immigration.

### Figure 41.12 — Acaricide Resistance Selection
Conceptual population sequence showing susceptible mites removed and resistant survivors contributing disproportionately after repeated selection.

### Figure 41.13 — Honey-Super and Temperature Decision Check
Pre-treatment checklist graphic: legal authorisation, supers, brood, forecast temperature, PPE, prior active ingredient and follow-up test.

### Figure 41.14 — Apiary Varroa Map
Sample apiary plan with colony-specific monitoring values, demonstrating why an average can hide one high-risk colony.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not depict Varroa as feeding only on haemolymph.
- Do not embed fixed universal thresholds in the artwork.
- Product illustrations must remain generic unless final publication rights and jurisdiction are explicitly defined.
- Treatment diagrams must never encourage off-label dose changes.
- Adult-bee sampling must clearly show queen exclusion.
- Seasonal graphs are examples, not fixed calendars.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, shapes and patterns as well as colour.
- Include readable scale cues.
- Make calculation figures understandable in greyscale.
- Captions must distinguish measurement from management decision.

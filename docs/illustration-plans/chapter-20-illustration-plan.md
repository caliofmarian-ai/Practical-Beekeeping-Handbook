Sharp edges, loose handles, damaged electrics, cracked food-grade plastic and corroded metal examples.

## Accuracy Requirements
- Do not include optional gadgetry as mandatory beginner equipment.
- Food-contact equipment must be visibly distinct from dirty field tools.

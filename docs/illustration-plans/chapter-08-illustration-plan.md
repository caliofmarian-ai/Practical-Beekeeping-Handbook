## Planned Figures

### Figure 8.1 — Queen Anatomy and Identification
Queen among workers with elongated abdomen, thoracic proportions and marking location.

### Figure 8.2 — Queen Reproductive System
Ovaries, oviducts, spermatheca and reproductive tract in simplified anatomical form.

### Figure 8.3 — Mating and Sperm Storage Concept
Virgin queen mating flights → multiple matings → spermathecal storage → fertilised worker/queen eggs versus unfertilised drone eggs.

Conceptual network showing queen signals affecting worker behaviour and colony organisation without implying one pheromone controls everything.

### Figure 8.5 — Queen-Life Management Timeline
Emergence, maturation, mating, laying, performance monitoring, supersedure/replacement.

## Accuracy Requirements
- Avoid anthropomorphic “ruler” imagery.
- Reproductive anatomy must be anatomically accurate and educational rather than decorative.

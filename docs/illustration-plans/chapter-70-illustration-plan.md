## Accuracy Requirements

- The EU 3 km example must be labelled jurisdiction- and date-specific.
- Do not portray the 3 km radius as the biological maximum flight range.
- Organic permission for an active substance must not imply arbitrary homemade use is legally authorised.
- Do not portray organic as zero-residue, zero-carbon, or automatically biodiversity-positive.
- Welfare treatment must take priority over preserving marketing status.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, patterns and shapes in addition to colour.
- Maps must include a legend and not rely on red/green only.
- Regulatory examples need clear captions stating that requirements vary by jurisdiction.

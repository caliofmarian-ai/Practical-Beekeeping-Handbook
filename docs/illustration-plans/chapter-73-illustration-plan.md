## Accuracy Requirements

- Evidence hierarchy must not imply randomised trials are appropriate for every research question.
- A p-value must not be illustrated as probability that the hypothesis is true.
- AI accuracy must always be paired with test-set design and error types.
- Prototype technologies must not be shown as validated routine tools.
- Laboratory antimicrobial/antiviral findings must not be presented as field efficacy.
- Research-frontier figures must remain date-sensitive and suitable for future updating.

## Accessibility

- Use text labels and patterns in addition to colour.
- Statistical figures must work in greyscale.
- Technical AI/research concepts should include plain-language captions.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Saleable comb must be depicted as freshly built and broodless.
- Foundation remaining in edible comb must be shown as appropriate beeswax where required by the governing product definition.
- Do not imply white/dry cappings prove low moisture or authenticity.
- Do not hard-code universal freezer times or temperatures.
- No pesticide spray should be shown around exposed food comb.
- Fresh comb must be depicted as mechanically vulnerable to heat and rough handling.
- Moisture sampling should be representative and should not imply every section must be destroyed.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, textures, arrows, and patterns in addition to colour.
- Defect panels must remain interpretable in greyscale.
- Safety/pest-control graphics must not rely on red/green alone.
- Captions should explain the management lesson and its limitation.

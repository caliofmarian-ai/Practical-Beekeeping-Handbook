# Illustration Plan — Chapter 37

## Chapter

Chapter 37 — Common Beginner Mistakes

## Purpose

This document defines the professional visual material to be produced during the final illustration and layout stage. No artwork is inserted into the manuscript during drafting.

## Proposed Illustrations

### Illustration 37.1 — Inspection Mistakes

**Type:** Comparative field plate

**Panels:**

- correct frame handling;
- crushed bees between boxes;
- excessive smoke;
- incorrect frame spacing;
- brood exposed in unsuitable weather.

**Purpose:** Show avoidable handling errors and correct alternatives.

### Illustration 37.2 — Space Management Errors

**Type:** Three-stage comparison

**Content:**

- excessive space on a weak colony;
- correctly matched space;
- congested colony with insufficient space.

### Illustration 37.3 — Queen-Cell Decision Guide

**Type:** Decision-flow graphic

**Required content:**

- queen present or absent;
- eggs and young brood;
- queen-cell location;
- swarm, supersedure, or emergency context;
- warning against automatic destruction.

### Illustration 37.4 — Feeding and Robbing Risks

**Type:** Cause-and-effect infographic

**Content:**

- spilled syrup;
- leaking feeder;
- exposed wet supers;
- wide entrance on weak colony;
- robbing response.

### Illustration 37.5 — Delayed Parasite Management

**Type:** Conceptual seasonal chart

**Purpose:** Show how a colony can appear strong while parasite damage rises and later becomes visible.

### Illustration 37.6 — Harvest Mistakes

**Type:** Food-safety comparison plate

**Panels:**

- unripe honey;
- dirty equipment;
- unsuitable container;
- missing batch code;
- correct hygienic handling.

### Illustration 37.7 — Mistake Correction Cycle

**Type:** Circular management infographic

**Stages:**

1. stop harmful action;
2. assess urgency;
3. identify cause;
4. protect colony;
5. choose correction;
6. record;
7. verify;
8. prevent recurrence.

## Tables Recommended for Final Layout

- common mistake, consequence, warning sign, prevention, correction;
- pre-inspection checklist;
- hive-closing checklist;
- urgent versus non-urgent problem matrix;
- seasonal mistake-prevention checklist.

## Visual Style

- publication quality;
- realistic hive equipment;
- clear correct-versus-incorrect comparisons;
- no exaggerated bee behaviour;
- no unsafe handling;
- concise labels;
- consistent protective clothing;
- neutral backgrounds;
- no artwork implying universal legal treatment rules.

## Final Production Checks

- verify all examples against the final manuscript;
- confirm that incorrect practices are clearly identified;
- ensure no unsafe action appears without correction;
- coordinate with Chapters 25–36;
- maintain consistent hive architecture;
- confirm numbering during final layout.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not depict one global propolis formula or one colour as universal quality.
- Trap propolis is cleaner by design but must not be shown as automatically contaminant-free.
- Solvent figures must show that extracts differ chemically.
- No therapeutic or disease-treatment claims in artwork.
- In-vitro antimicrobial/antioxidant activity must remain separate from clinical efficacy.
- Ethanol figures must prioritise fire/ventilation controls and omit DIY heating instructions.
- Allergy/contact-dermatitis risk must be visible.
- Draft ISO extract work must not be labelled as a final published standard.

## Accessibility

- Use patterns, labels, and symbols in addition to colour.
- Evidence ladder must remain legible in greyscale.
- Technical analytical abbreviations should have plain-language caption explanations.
- Safety illustrations must not rely on red/green colour alone.

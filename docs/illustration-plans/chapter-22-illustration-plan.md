## Accuracy Requirements

- Smoke must never be portrayed as anaesthetising bees or eliminating sting risk.
- Smoke shown entering the colony must be cool, not visibly flaming or spark-producing.
- Fire-safety artwork must not imply that a heat shield makes placement on dry vegetation safe.
- Unsuitable fuels must include treated/painted/synthetic/oily materials.
- Hive tools must be shown using controlled leverage, not force that damages boxes or frames.
- Hygiene figures must not imply one disinfectant method is universally effective for every bee pathogen.

## Accessibility and Layout

- Use labels, symbols and patterns in addition to colour.
- Safety/unsafe comparisons must remain clear in greyscale.
- Smoker airflow arrows should be simple and mechanically accurate.
- Captions should explain the management lesson and relevant safety limitation.

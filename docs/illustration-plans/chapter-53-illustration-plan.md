## Accuracy Requirements

- Creamed honey must not be shown as containing dairy cream by definition.
- Do not portray air as the structure-forming element.
- Do not hard-code one seed percentage or one setting temperature as universally correct.
- High-moisture or fermented honey must not be shown as “fixed” by creaming.
- Added ingredients must trigger correct composite-food/allergen controls.
- White wall streaks must be distinguished from mould/fermentation unless other evidence supports spoilage.

## Accessibility

- Use labels and patterns in addition to colour.
- Crystal-size diagrams need scale cues.
- Defect panels should include text labels so they remain understandable in greyscale.

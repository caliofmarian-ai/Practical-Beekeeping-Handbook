# Illustration Plan — Chapter 35

## Chapter

Chapter 35 — Monthly Management Tasks

## Purpose

This document defines the professional visual material to be produced during the final illustration and layout stage. Artwork is not inserted into the manuscript during drafting.

## Proposed Illustrations

### Illustration 35.1 — Twelve-Month Management Wheel

**Type:** Circular operational calendar

**Purpose:** Present the principal practical priorities for all twelve months.

**Required content:**

- one clearly labelled segment per month;
- colony work;
- health monitoring;
- equipment preparation;
- food assessment;
- production or winter preparation;
- note explaining that timing varies locally.

### Illustration 35.2 — Monthly Priority Matrix

**Type:** Professional matrix

**Rows:**

- colony inspections;
- feeding;
- swarm control;
- queen management;
- health monitoring;
- super management;
- harvesting;
- winter preparation;
- equipment repair;
- record keeping.

**Columns:** January through December.

**Accuracy requirement:** Use generalized intensity levels rather than universal dates.

### Illustration 35.3 — One-Month-Ahead Equipment Planning

**Type:** Workflow infographic

**Purpose:** Demonstrate that equipment should be prepared before the biological need.

**Examples:**

- swarm equipment prepared before swarm season;
- supers prepared before nectar flow;
- extraction equipment prepared before harvest;
- feeders and pest guards prepared before autumn.

### Illustration 35.4 — Monthly Inspection Record

**Type:** Annotated professional record sheet

**Fields:**

- colony identification;
- date;
- weather;
- strength;
- brood;
- stores;
- queen evidence;
- health;
- action;
- next inspection trigger.

### Illustration 35.5 — Northern and Southern Month Conversion

**Type:** Dual annual timeline

**Purpose:** Show the approximate six-month shift while emphasizing biological adaptation.

### Illustration 35.6 — Monthly Task Priority System

**Type:** Three-level management graphic

**Levels:**

- critical;
- time-sensitive;
- routine.

**Purpose:** Help beekeepers allocate labour and avoid delaying high-risk work.

## Tables Recommended for Final Layout

- twelve-month operational summary;
- Northern and Southern Hemisphere conversion table;
- monthly equipment-readiness matrix;
- monthly health-record template;
- annual queen-performance summary.

## Visual Style

- scientifically accurate;
- clean publication layout;
- consistent typography;
- realistic hive equipment;
- concise labels;
- clear monthly navigation;
- accessible contrast;
- no unsafe handling;
- no region-specific assumption unless identified.

## Final Production Checks

- confirm monthly wording against the final manuscript;
- confirm all dates are described as adaptable;
- verify hemisphere conversion;
- verify that the health matrix does not imply unauthorized treatment schedules;
- maintain consistency with Chapter 34;
- ensure no graphic replaces local veterinary or regulatory advice;
- confirm numbering during final layout.

# Chapter 30 — Splitting Colonies: Illustration Plan

## Status

Planned for the final illustration and layout stage.

## Purpose

The visuals should explain resource balancing, queen strategies, flying-bee drift and establishment of viable splits.

## Visual 1 — Resources Required for a Split

Show:

- Adult bees.
- nurse bees.
- open brood.
- sealed brood.
- food.
- pollen.
- empty comb.
- queen or queen plan.
- reduced hive space.

## Visual 2 — Donor Colony Assessment

Show a brood box with:

- Healthy brood.
- food.
- adult-bee coverage.
- queen location.
- disease warning signs.
- frames suitable or unsuitable for transfer.

## Visual 3 — Queen Strategy Comparison

Compare:

- Original queen.
- mated queen.
- queen cell.
- virgin queen.
- walk-away queen rearing.

Include expected speed, risk and brood interruption.

## Visual 4 — Equal and Unequal Splits

Show:

- Equal division.
- small nucleus removed from a strong donor.
- differences in brood, bees and food.

## Visual 5 — Walk-Away Split

Show:

- Queen remaining in parent colony.
- eggs and young larvae moved.
- nurse bees.
- food.
- emergency queen-cell construction.

## Visual 6 — Mated-Queen Split

Show:

- Queen cage.
- queenless split.
- controlled release.
- worker acceptance behaviour.

## Visual 7 — Queen-Cell Split

Show:

- Correct mature queen cell.
- upright handling.
- placement between brood frames.
- prohibited shaking and inversion.

## Visual 8 — Brood Balance

Compare:

- Too much open brood with too few bees.
- balanced open and sealed brood.
- emerging brood strengthening the split.

## Visual 9 — Flying-Bee Drift

Show:

- Parent and split in same apiary.
- foragers returning to original stand.
- extra nurse bees remaining in split.
- alternate stand-position method.

## Visual 10 — Same-Apiary and Remote Movement

Compare:

- Split kept nearby with reduced entrance.
- split moved several kilometres.
- ventilated transport box.
- orientation branches.

## Visual 11 — Hive Volume

Compare:

- Small split in nucleus box.
- small split lost in excessive space.
- growing split transferred to larger box.

## Visual 12 — Queen Development Timeline

Show:

- Egg.
- larva.
- open queen cell.
- sealed queen cell.
- emergence.
- maturation.
- mating flights.
- first eggs.

## Visual 13 — First Inspection

Show checks for:

- Queen cells.
- emerged cell.
- eggs.
- worker brood.
- stores.
- robbing.
- population.

## Visual 14 — Failed Split Decision

Show options:

- Add mated queen.
- add queen cell.
- add young brood.
- strengthen.
- combine.
- reduce hive volume.

## Visual 15 — Overwintering Split

Show:

- Adequate bee population.
- food arrangement.
- reduced entrance.
- insulation.
- Varroa management.
- queen status.

## Style Direction

- Professional scientific handbook style.
- Accurate brood stages.
- Consistent hive equipment.
- Clear resource labels.
- Natural colours.
- No commercial branding.
- No cartoon anthropomorphism.
- Suitable for print and digital publication.

## Production Notes

All visuals should be created during final layout with consistent typography, line weight, captions, dimensions and colour treatment.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Centrifugal force and frame orientation must be mechanically accurate.
- New/foundationless comb must not be depicted as safe at the same aggressive speed as strong established comb.
- Avoid fixed universal warming temperatures; time/temperature management is product- and system-dependent.
- Do not depict pollen as an impurity that must always be removed.
- Cleaning figures must show complete drying before honey contact.
- Guard/interlock safety must not be optional in motorised-equipment artwork.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, arrows, symbols and patterns in addition to colour.
- Floor plans and flow diagrams must remain clear in greyscale.
- Captions should state the operational principle and the failure mode being prevented.

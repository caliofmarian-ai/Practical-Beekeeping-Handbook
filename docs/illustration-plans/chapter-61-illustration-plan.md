## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not include static catalogue prices as universal values.
- Used brood comb must visibly carry disease/residue caution.
- Capacity figures must be clearly illustrative, not product recommendations.
- Depreciation examples must be described as management estimates; tax treatment varies by jurisdiction.
- Manual-handling and safety investments must be treated as real economic benefits, not optional decoration.
- Imported electrical/food-contact equipment must include compliance/documentation check.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, patterns and symbols in addition to colour.
- Financial graphs must remain readable in greyscale.
- Decision trees should use explicit yes/no text rather than colour-only paths.

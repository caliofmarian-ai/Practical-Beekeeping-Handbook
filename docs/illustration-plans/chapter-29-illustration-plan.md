# Chapter 29 — Swarm Capture: Illustration Plan

## Status

Planned for the final illustration and layout stage.

## Purpose

The visuals should show safe swarm assessment, collection, transport, installation and passive capture through bait hives.

## Style Direction

- Professional scientific handbook style.
- Accurate honey bee proportions.
- Realistic protective equipment.
- Clear hazard communication.
- Consistent hive components.
- Natural colours.
- No commercial branding.
- No cartoon anthropomorphism.
- Suitable for print and digital publication.

## Production Notes

All visuals should be created during final layout with consistent typography, line weight, captions, dimensions and colour treatment.

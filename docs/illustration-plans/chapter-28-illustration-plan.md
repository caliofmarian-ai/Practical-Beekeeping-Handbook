# Chapter 28 — Swarm Prevention: Illustration Plan

## Status

Planned for the final illustration and layout stage.

## Purpose

The visuals should help readers identify swarm preparation, understand congestion and apply prevention methods accurately.

## Visual 1 — Natural Swarm Cycle

Show:

- Strong parent colony.
- queen-cell development.
- old queen departing with workers.
- swarm clustering temporarily.
- parent colony retaining brood and queen cells.
- new queen emerging.

## Visual 2 — Congestion Versus Empty Volume

Compare:

- A colony with an empty unused super but a congested brood nest.
- A colony with drawn usable comb correctly positioned.

Emphasise that empty volume is not automatically usable space.

## Visual 3 — Brood-Nest Restriction

Show:

- Balanced brood frame.
- nectar-bound brood frame.
- pollen-bound brood frame.
- reduced polished cells available to the queen.

## Visual 4 — Queen Cup Development

Show sequentially:

1. Empty queen cup.
2. egg in cup.
3. young larva with royal jelly.
4. advanced open queen cell.
5. sealed queen cell.

## Visual 5 — Queen-Cell Types

Compare:

- Swarm cells.
- supersedure cells.
- emergency queen cells.

Include typical positions but clearly state that colony context is required.

## Visual 6 — Early, Intermediate and Advanced Preparation

Show three colony states:

- Early congestion and empty cups.
- occupied cells and reduced laying space.
- sealed cells and slimmed queen.

## Visual 7 — Effective Supering

Compare:

- Early addition of drawn comb.
- late addition of foundation after sealed cells.

## Visual 8 — Opening the Brood Nest

Show:

- Correct placement of one drawn comb in a strong colony.
- incorrect division with multiple cold frames.

## Visual 9 — Nucleus Formation

Show donor colony resources removed:

- brood.
- adhering bees.
- food.
- suitable queen plan.

Show resulting reduction of congestion.

## Visual 10 — Artificial Swarm

Show:

- Original stand.
- new hive with old queen and returning flying bees.
- moved brood hive with queen cells.
- flight direction.

## Visual 11 — Demaree-Type Arrangement

Show vertical hive layers:

- queen below with empty drawn comb.
- queen excluder.
- supers.
- brood above.
- queen-cell inspection points.

## Visual 12 — Horizontal Hive Expansion

Show:

- brood nest.
- food barrier.
- correct lateral placement of empty drawn comb.
- follower boards.
- possible nucleus compartment.

## Visual 13 — Post-Intervention Inspection

Show checklist observations:

- eggs.
- queen cells.
- empty laying space.
- super use.
- colony population.
- next inspection date.

## Style Direction

- Professional scientific handbook style.
- Accurate bee and comb proportions.
- Consistent hive components.
- Clear labels.
- Natural colours.
- No commercial branding.
- No cartoon anthropomorphism.
- Suitable for print and digital publication.

## Production Notes

All visuals should be created during final layout with consistent typography, line weight, caption system, dimensions and colour treatment.

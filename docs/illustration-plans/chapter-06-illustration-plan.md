## Planned Figures

Head, thorax, abdomen, antennae, compound eyes, ocelli, mouthparts, legs, wings and sting region.

### Figure 6.2 — Head and Mouthparts
Antenna, mandibles, proboscis and major sensory structures with functional labels.

### Figure 6.3 — Leg Specialisations
Foreleg antenna cleaner, middle leg and hind-leg pollen basket/press structures.

### Figure 6.4 — Internal Systems
Simplified digestive tract, crop, midgut, Malpighian tubules, tracheae, dorsal vessel, nervous system and major glands.

Relative abdomen, eyes, reproductive structures and functional differences; not merely size silhouettes.

## Accuracy Requirements
- Anatomical proportions and terminology must follow authoritative entomological references.
- Use magnified insets instead of making microscopic structures appear naturally large.

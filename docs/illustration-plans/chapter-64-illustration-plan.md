## Accuracy Requirements

- EU distance-selling rules must be labelled as jurisdiction-specific and current to the edition date.
- Online information must not be shown as replacing physical mandatory labels at delivery.
- HOLD/REJECTED inventory must never be shown as sellable.
- Raw traffic/revenue must not be portrayed as profit.
- Frozen/chilled product graphics must show validated cold-chain logistics.
- Consumer/privacy rights should be stated generally; exact return exceptions vary by product/jurisdiction.
- Payment-card data should be shown handled by appropriate secure payment systems rather than casually stored by the seller.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, icons and patterns in addition to colour.
- Funnel charts must remain readable in greyscale.
- Checkout/product-page mock-up must use realistic mobile-sized text hierarchy.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not depict honey as sterile.
- Do not imply HACCP means every step is a CCP.
- Product risk must differ realistically between mature honey and high-moisture hive products.
- Cleaning and sanitation must remain distinct.
- Food-safety, quality and marketing defects must not be conflated.
- Emergency figures must show segregation before rework or release.

## Accessibility

- Use text labels, icons and patterns in addition to colour.
- HOLD/RELEASE states must not depend on red/green alone.
- Flow diagrams should remain readable in greyscale and at print-page scale.

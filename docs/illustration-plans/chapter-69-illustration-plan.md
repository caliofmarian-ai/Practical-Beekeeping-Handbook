## Accuracy Requirements

- Insurance must never be depicted as a substitute for food safety or legal compliance.
- Public and product liability must be clearly distinguished.
- Recall cover must not be implied to be included automatically in product liability.
- Legal requirement, contract requirement and prudent voluntary cover must remain separate.
- No universal indemnity limit should be presented as appropriate for every business.
- Claims-made/occurrence illustrations must be clearly labelled as general concepts subject to policy wording.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, icons and patterns in addition to colour.
- Policy layers must be readable in greyscale.
- Risk maps should use plain-language captions suitable for non-insurance specialists.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Package, nucleus and swarm must not be portrayed as biologically equivalent starting units.
- Nucleus transfer must preserve brood-frame order/orientation unless a specific management reason requires change.
- Queen introduction artwork must not hard-code one release time or method as universally correct.
- Transport figure must prioritise ventilation and heat control; no sealed populous bee container.
- Feeding visuals must avoid exposing syrup/honey and must note disease risk from unknown honey.
- Swarm/new-colony imagery must not imply parasite or disease freedom simply because brood is initially absent.

## Accessibility and Layout

- Use consistent hive orientation and frame geometry across figures.
- Numbered procedures must remain readable in greyscale.
- Captions should identify what differs among starting units and which decisions depend on local/supplier guidance.

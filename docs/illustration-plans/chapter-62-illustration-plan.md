## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not imply likes or followers equal profitable demand.
- Customer segmentation must be based on meaningful needs/behaviours, not stereotypes.
- EU/Irish legal examples must be clearly dated and labelled jurisdiction-specific.
- Honey must not be portrayed with unauthorised health or disease-treatment claims.
- Floral and regional claims must show supporting evidence/traceability.
- Channel comparison must include hidden costs, not only selling price.
- Marketing metrics must define the denominator where relevant.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, patterns, icons and shapes in addition to colour.
- Funnel and channel charts must remain readable in greyscale.
- Legal-claim boundaries must use explicit text, not colour-only safe/unsafe coding.

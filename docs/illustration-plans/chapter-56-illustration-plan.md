## Accuracy Requirements

- Do not depict pollen trapping as removing all incoming pollen.
- Bee bread must not be shown as identical to fresh pollen pellets.
- No fixed universal trap schedule should be illustrated.
- Drying figures must avoid one universal temperature/time recipe.
- Mouldy pollen must not be portrayed as recoverable simply by later drying.
- Pollen colour must not be used as stand-alone botanical proof.
- Antioxidant/nutrient graphics must not imply authorised therapeutic benefit.
- Allergy risk must be visible and serious.

## Accessibility

- Use labels, patterns, and symbols in addition to colour.
- Multi-colour pollen graphics must include text labels for readers with colour-vision deficiencies.
- Safety figures must not rely on red/green coding alone.
- Captions should distinguish colony biology, food quality, and consumer-health risk.

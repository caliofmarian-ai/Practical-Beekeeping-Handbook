## Accuracy Requirements

- No figure may imply that one country's requirements apply globally.
- Irish/EU examples must be visibly time-dated and jurisdiction-labelled.
- Business-name registration and trade-mark protection must remain separate.
- Insurance must not be shown as proof of legal compliance.
- Health/medicinal claims must show potential reclassification, not a marketing shortcut.
- Notifiable-disease figures must prioritise competent-authority instructions over routine management.

## Accessibility

- Use labels and icons in addition to colour.
- Compliance matrices and layered diagrams must remain readable in greyscale.
- Jurisdiction/date notes should appear in captions, not hidden in fine print.

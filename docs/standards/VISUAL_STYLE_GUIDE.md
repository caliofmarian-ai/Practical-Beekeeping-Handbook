# Visual Style Guide

**Status:** Canonical  
**Scope:** all diagrams, technical illustrations, photographs, process maps, tables-as-figures, and final-layout visuals.

## Purpose

Colour must improve comprehension and memory without becoming decoration. Every visual must remain understandable in greyscale, at final print size, and on a phone screen.

## Canonical semantic palette

| Meaning | Primary | Light fill | Dark accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Correct / healthy / recommended | `#2E7D32` | `#E8F5E9` | `#1B5E20` |
| Danger / disease / prohibited | `#C62828` | `#FFEBEE` | `#8E0000` |
| Warning / caution / intermediate risk | `#EF6C00` | `#FFF3E0` | `#E65100` |
| Neutral process / workflow | `#1565C0` | `#E3F2FD` | `#0D47A1` |
| Laboratory / analysis / diagnostics | `#6A1B9A` | `#F3E5F5` | `#4A148C` |
| Honey / wax / natural hive products | `#D4A017` | `#FFF8E1` | `#8D6E63` |
| Secondary / inactive context | `#757575` | `#F5F5F5` | `#424242` |

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Meaning must never depend on colour alone.** Use labels, icons, line style, border weight, shape, and position as redundant cues.
2. **Healthy vs abnormal:** green may reinforce healthy/recommended states; red may reinforce danger/disease; orange indicates caution or uncertainty.
3. **Neutral procedure:** blue is the default workflow colour.
4. **Laboratory/diagnostic:** purple identifies sampling, microscopy, PCR/qPCR, laboratory interpretation, and verification.
5. **Natural materials:** honey, wax, pollen, propolis, royal jelly and product-processing visuals may use restrained natural gold/cream/brown palettes.
6. **Biological accuracy wins over branding.** Morphology, diagnostic colour, anatomy, scale, and texture must not be recoloured inaccurately for style.
7. **High-fidelity biological plates** require morphology/diagnostic review and are not forced into simple schematic colour conventions.
8. **Safety warnings** must remain visible in greyscale and at thumbnail size.

## Figure-family conventions

### Safety and first aid
- Workflow: blue.
- Correct practice: green.
- Caution: orange.
- Immediate danger/prohibited action: red.
- PPE is a layer of control, never a guarantee.

### Hive installation, inspection and transport
- Process: blue.
- Successful/verified state: green.
- Stop/check/review: orange.
- Emergency/failure: red.

### Bee health and disease
- Diagnostic/lab workflow: purple.
- Healthy reference: green only when the biology supports that label.
- Disease/hazard: red.
- Differential/uncertain findings: orange.
- Do not make a non-specific sign look diagnostic through colour emphasis.

### Honey and hive products
- Natural materials: honey gold, wax cream, restrained browns.
- Food-safety process: blue.
- Compliant/released: green.
- Hold/caution: orange.
- Contaminated/rejected: red.
- Lab verification: purple.

### Business, traceability and legal
- Process: blue.
- Verified/compliant: green.
- Review/hold: orange.
- Non-compliant: red.
- Audit/lab/document verification: purple.

## Typography and line work

- Use clear sans-serif type.
- Titles and labels must remain legible at final printed size.
- Prefer dark text on light fills.
- Avoid long paragraphs inside figures.
- Use a limited line-weight system: primary outline, secondary detail, light guide.
- Arrows must be consistent within a figure.

## Greyscale support

Colour-coded states must also differ by one or more of:
- icon;
- border weight;
- dashed/solid line;
- shape;
- text label;
- position.

Recommended:
- correct = check icon / solid border;
- danger = cross or warning icon / heavy border;
- caution = dashed border / exclamation icon;
- lab = distinctive header band or microscope/test icon.

## Accessibility

Every final visual must:
- include meaningful alt text;
- use sufficient contrast;
- avoid red/green-only distinctions;
- be readable at phone width and final print width;
- preserve semantic meaning when printed in greyscale.

## Approval gate

A visual passes colour review only when:
- [ ] colour has an instructional purpose;
- [ ] canonical semantics are respected;
- [ ] labels remain readable;
- [ ] figure works in greyscale;
- [ ] figure works at thumbnail size;
- [ ] biological/mechanical accuracy is unchanged;
- [ ] safety/legal meaning is not weakened;
- [ ] visual family remains consistent;
- [ ] final layout proof is still pending or passed explicitly.

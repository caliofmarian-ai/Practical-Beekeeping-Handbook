# Illustration Standards

**Project:** Practical Beekeeping Handbook

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Illustrations shall help readers understand concepts that are difficult to explain using text alone.

They are educational tools, not decorative elements.

---

# Types of Illustrations

The handbook may include:

- Technical drawings
- Cross-sections
- Hive diagrams
- Bee anatomy
- Equipment diagrams
- Seasonal workflows
- Decision trees
- Maps
- Infographics

---

# Style

All illustrations shall:

- Use a consistent visual style
- Be easy to read
- Avoid unnecessary decoration
- Clearly label important parts
- Match the terminology used in the text

---

# Numbering

Examples:

Figure 1.1

Figure 2.3

Diagram 4.2

---

# Captions

Every illustration must include:

- Figure number
- Short title
- Brief explanation

---

# Originality

Original illustrations are preferred.

If external material is used, the licence and attribution must be verified.

---

# Quality Checklist

Before publication every illustration must be checked for:

- Accuracy
- Readability
- Consistent terminology
- Correct numbering
- Proper caption
- Sufficient resolution

# Controlled Photograph Capture Brief — A_CORE Set

**Issue:** #102 — Reference 84 — Photographs  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Scope:** eight `CONTROLLED_REAL_CAPTURE` A_CORE photographs  
**State:** CAPTURE_READY — final photographs not yet created

## Purpose

This brief converts the controlled-capture portion of the A_CORE register into an executable shot plan. Every final asset must be a real photograph. Generative imagery, undisclosed compositing, invented labels and misleading product staging are prohibited.

## Common capture standard

- Capture RAW plus highest-quality JPEG where the camera permits it.
- Minimum preferred source size: 3000 px on the long edge; retain the uncropped original.
- Use neutral, colour-stable light and include a grey card in a separate calibration frame.
- Avoid visible brands unless editorially necessary and cleared for publication.
- Do not display personal, patient, customer, supplier, location or account data.
- Record creator, date, location class, equipment, consent/property release, edits and rights owner.
- Permitted edits: crop, rotation, exposure, white balance, modest contrast, dust removal and redaction of incidental private data. Record every material edit.
- Final approval requires technical review, caption/alt text, commercial print/digital rights, placement and proof verification.

## Shot cards

### P22.02 — Hive Tools

**Learning purpose:** identify a small, representative tool set and distinguish serviceable condition from unsafe damage.  
**Setup:** overhead or 30-degree view on a matte neutral surface; clean hive tool, frame grip if included, scraper/brush only where genuinely used; one separately positioned damaged example may be shown.  
**Required cues:** full tool silhouettes, working edges, scale reference, no commercial clutter.  
**Safety:** blunt/cover sharp edges during setup; do not imply contaminated tools are ready for use.  
**Acceptance:** tools remain identifiable at single-column size; wear/damage is explained by caption rather than colour alone.

### P23.01 — First-Aid / Emergency Preparedness Kit

**Learning purpose:** show organised preparedness without presenting a universal medical prescription.  
**Setup:** open kit with clearly separated communication, identification, basic wound-care and emergency-plan elements; region-specific medicines remain optional and must not be staged as universally appropriate.  
**Required cues:** emergency contact card with fictional data, charged phone or communication symbol, visible expiry-check cue.  
**Safety:** no patient scene, needle use or treatment demonstration; caption directs readers to local medical guidance and individual emergency plans.  
**Acceptance:** no private data, expired supplies, dosage claims or implication that the kit replaces emergency services.

### P48.02 — Refractometer Use

**Learning purpose:** document clean sampling and reading technique, not endorse a brand or invent a result.  
**Setup:** clean refractometer, representative honey sample, disposable transfer tool and lint-free cleaning material; capture sample placement and reading as separate frames if one image becomes crowded.  
**Required cues:** clean prism, small representative sample, temperature/context note in caption, readable display only when the value is genuine.  
**Safety:** food-contact hygiene; no return of tested sample to sale stock.  
**Acceptance:** actual reading is recorded with instrument identity/calibration context; no fabricated display or universal pass/fail threshold.

### P55.02 — Cappings Wax and Brood Wax Blocks

**Learning purpose:** compare real source streams without implying colour alone proves purity or quality.  
**Setup:** separately labelled cappings-wax and brood-comb-wax blocks from traceable sources, photographed together under identical light with a neutral scale.  
**Required cues:** labels remain adjacent but physically separate from product; texture and visible debris retained honestly.  
**Safety:** cooled stable blocks; no hot-wax handling in the final frame.  
**Acceptance:** provenance is documented; caption states that appearance alone does not establish purity, residues or fitness for use.

### P56.02 — Cleaned/Dried or Frozen Pollen Product

**Learning purpose:** show a real post-harvest product state and protective packaging.  
**Setup:** traceable bee pollen in a clean food-contact container; choose one accurately documented preservation state rather than visually conflating dried and frozen product.  
**Required cues:** batch/handling identifier using fictional or publication-safe data, sealed/covered storage, colour variation retained.  
**Safety:** food hygiene and allergen controls; no health claim or tasting scene.  
**Acceptance:** preservation method and capture condition are recorded; caption does not infer safety or botanical origin from appearance.

### P57.02 — Raw Propolis Grades

**Learning purpose:** compare honestly defined raw-material grades or source fractions.  
**Setup:** two or more traceable lots in identical neutral trays with physical labels and scale; grading basis must be recorded before capture.  
**Required cues:** visible wax/wood/debris differences where genuinely present; no cosmetic cleaning that changes the represented grade.  
**Safety:** gloves where appropriate; allergen/sensitisation caution in production notes.  
**Acceptance:** grade names have an explicit project or market definition; colour is not used as the sole quality claim.

### P58.02 — Royal Jelly Cold Chain

**Learning purpose:** show containment, identification and temperature-control workflow without making a shelf-life claim.  
**Setup:** sealed traceable container inside an insulated transport/storage system with a genuine temperature-monitoring device; capture the displayed value only if documented at the same moment.  
**Required cues:** closed container, batch-safe label, monitor/probe relationship and protective secondary containment.  
**Safety:** food hygiene; avoid prolonged warming merely for photography.  
**Acceptance:** time and temperature are recorded; caption avoids universal storage duration and does not imply that one reading proves the full cold chain.

### P59.02 — Dried Bee Venom Sample

**Learning purpose:** document a real, controlled sample and its containment, not demonstrate collection or medical use.  
**Setup:** only a legally held, traceable sample in its closed primary container with secondary containment; use a non-sensitive publication identifier.  
**Required cues:** closed container, scale, hazard/handling context where applicable; no loose powder staged for visual drama.  
**Safety:** authorised handler and local risk assessment required; no opening, inhalation exposure, skin contact, injection or self-treatment scene.  
**Acceptance:** provenance and permissions are documented; caption clearly separates raw venom from regulated medicinal venom immunotherapy.

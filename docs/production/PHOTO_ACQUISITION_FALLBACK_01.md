# Photograph Acquisition Fallback — Remaining Hard-to-Source A_CORE Subjects

**Issue:** #102 — Reference 84 — Photographs  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**State:** ACQUISITION_READY — final photographs do not yet exist

## Purpose

After five sourcing passes, only three A_CORE subjects have neither a usable source candidate nor an existing controlled-capture card. This brief defines the safe fallback so the project does not accept weak stock imagery, unclear rights, staged damage, or AI-generated documentary photographs.

## P19.02 — Old Brood Comb

**Preferred acquisition:** controlled real capture from a beekeeper's legitimately retired brood comb.  
**Learning purpose:** show darkening/cocoon accumulation and real use history without implying that colour alone proves contamination or disease.  
**Setup:** retired brood comb photographed vertically beside a clearly newer brood/honey comb only if provenance is known; neutral light and scale reference.  
**Required metadata:** approximate service history if known, apiary-source class, reason for retirement, photographer/rights owner.  
**Do not:** artificially stain comb, burn/darken wax, or label age from colour alone.  
**Acceptance:** real comb, full-frame detail readable at print size, rights owned/cleared, caption explains that visual darkness is not by itself a residue or disease diagnosis.

## P38.02 — Storm-Damaged Hive

**Preferred acquisition:** documentary-only real event photograph commissioned from a beekeeper, association, insurer, extension service or project contributor after naturally occurring damage.  
**Learning purpose:** show realistic displacement/water/wind damage and the need to assess safety before colony recovery.  
**Do not:** damage a hive for photography, expose bees to weather, or create a misleading staged “storm scene.”  
**Required cues:** enough surrounding context to distinguish weather damage from ordinary dismantling or transport; no unsafe rescue behaviour staged.  
**Rights/privacy:** property permission and location/privacy review where necessary.  
**Acceptance:** real event provenance, commercial print/digital rights, sufficient resolution, caption avoids inferring colony survival or cause beyond what is documented.

## P52.02 — Comb-Honey Defect Examples

**Preferred acquisition:** controlled multi-photo plate using real rejected/down-graded comb-honey samples from normal production.  
**Learning purpose:** compare genuine defects such as incomplete sections, travel stain, pollen/propolis intrusion or heat/handling damage without fabricating defects.  
**Setup:** identical neutral background, scale, light and crop across specimens; each specimen physically separated and traceable.  
**Required metadata:** defect/source description, whether naturally occurring or handling-related, capture date, photographer/rights owner.  
**Do not:** contaminate edible product deliberately, add propolis/pollen/staining for effect, or imply a universal legal grade.  
**Acceptance:** defects are real and visible at print size; caption distinguishes appearance/grading from food-safety conclusions.

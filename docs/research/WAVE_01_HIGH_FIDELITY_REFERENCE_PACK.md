# Wave 01 High-Fidelity Scientific Reference Pack

**Status:** production reference pack  
**Scope:** the 18 remaining A_CORE visuals intentionally excluded from low-detail vector treatment.

## Purpose

This pack translates current authoritative and peer-reviewed sources into illustration constraints. It is not a substitute for source reading. The goal is to keep morphology, brood-stage biology, diagnostic limitations, and disease-control messaging accurate before high-fidelity art is generated.

## Cross-cutting production rules

- Do not convert a suggestive sign into a visual diagnosis.
- Maintain realistic developmental stage, cell geometry, mite body shape and scale relationships.
- Show uncertainty explicitly where field signs overlap.
- Use original artwork or rights-cleared reference material only.
- When a source image is consulted, reproduce the biological concept, not the source composition.
- Add scale/magnification cues whenever relative size could be misunderstood.
- Preserve the canonical semantic colour system, but do not recolour diagnostic morphology unrealistically.
- Final captions must state what the image can and cannot establish.

## F25.07 — Brood Pattern Interpretation

**Target:** coherent healthy capped-worker brood versus irregular brood that triggers investigation.

**Reference constraints**
- Healthy capped worker brood should appear predominantly compact, with relatively few missed cells.
- Healthy larval reference should remain pearly white and glistening.
- Irregular brood is non-specific: queen performance, chilling, Varroa-associated removal, infectious disease and nutrition can all contribute.

**Production note**
Create two matched comb areas at the same apparent scale. Do not label the irregular panel with one disease name.

**Sources**
- FDA, *Helping Agriculture's Helpful Honey Bees*: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/helping-agricultures-helpful-honey-bees
- MAAREC, *The Colony and Its Organization*: https://canr.udel.edu/maarec/honey-bee-biology/the-colony-and-its-organization/

## F40.03 — Varroa Reproductive Cycle

**Target:** biologically realistic worker-brood cell sequence.

**Sequence**
1. foundress enters a suitable larval cell shortly before capping;
2. brood cell is capped;
3. foundress feeds and initiates reproduction;
4. first offspring is male, subsequent offspring female;
5. offspring develop through immature mite stages;
6. mating occurs within the capped cell;
7. mature daughters and foundress leave when the adult bee emerges.

**Constraint**
Do not freeze variable biological timing into a universal clock. If timing is shown, label it as approximate/contextual.

**Sources**
- USDA ARS, *Varroa Mite Life Cycle and Reproduction*: https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/tucson-az/carl-hayden-bee-research-center/research/varroa/varroa-mite-life-cycle-and-reproduction/
- Traynor et al. review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8825774/

## F40.04 — Varroa on Adult Bees

**Target:** adult worker plus mite position and feeding-site inset.

**Critical correction**
Do not teach the outdated “mite merely drinks haemolymph” model. The adult-bee feeding inset should identify fat-body tissue as a major feeding target beneath the cuticle.

**Placement**
Use realistic mite orientation and protected intersegmental/ventral abdominal positioning rather than an oversized dorsal decorative mite.

**Sources**
- Ramsey et al., PNAS 2019: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1818371116
- Open-access full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6358713/

## F40.08 — Tropilaelaps Life Cycle and Brood Dependence

**Target:** brood-cell cycle plus short adult-bee dispersal interval.

**Show**
- reproduction on developing brood;
- larval, protonymph, deutonymph and adult mite stages;
- emergence from brood cell;
- brief adult-bee-associated dispersal phase;
- rapid return to brood when brood is available.

**Current nuance**
Recent review literature describes strong brood dependence but also a brief adult-bee survival/dispersal interval; do not label adult-bee survival as zero.

**Sources**
- 2026 comprehensive review: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/insect-science/articles/10.3389/finsc.2026.1871985/full
- WOAH: https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahm/2.02.06_TROPILAELAPS.pdf

## F42.01 — Nosema Life Cycle in an Adult Bee

**Target:** microsporidian infection cycle focused on the midgut epithelium.

**Show**
1. ingestion of mature spores;
2. germination in the midgut;
3. polar-tube extrusion;
4. sporoplasm transfer into epithelial cell;
5. intracellular proliferative stages;
6. sporogony and formation of new spores;
7. release back to gut lumen / transmission route.

**Constraint**
Do not draw the polar tube as an ordinary bacterial flagellum or external worm-like organ.

**Sources**
- *Nosema Disease of European Honey Bees*: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8468538/
- Nosemosis biology/diagnostic review: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/12/5890

## F46.06 — Drone-Brood Removal Sequence

**Target:** mechanically clear IPM procedure concept.

**Show**
1. dedicated drone-comb area;
2. drone larvae/capping;
3. Varroa reproduction in capped drone brood;
4. removal before adult-drone emergence;
5. hygienic handling / destruction or processing according to the chosen legal management method;
6. continued monitoring.

**Critical warning**
Late removal after emergence can add mites rather than remove them. This visual must make the timing principle obvious without embedding a universal calendar date.

**Sources**
- National Bee Unit fact sheet on drone brood as Varroa control: https://irishbeekeeping.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fact_Sheet_14_Drone_Brood_Removal_FV.pdf
- Penn State IPM: https://extension.psu.edu/methods-to-control-varroa-mites-an-integrated-pest-management-approach

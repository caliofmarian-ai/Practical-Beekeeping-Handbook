## P58.02 — Royal Jelly Cold Chain

**Decision:** C_OPTIONAL / OMIT_V1_LOW_INFORMATION_VALUE

A generic photograph of a food-grade container entering refrigeration would add little information. The important teaching is explicit time/temperature control, clean handling, traceability and storage validation. Those requirements are better communicated by text and the existing quality-control visual system than by a refrigerator photograph.

## Product / medical controls

- **ISO 12824:2016 — Royal jelly — Specifications** remains current after confirmation; **ISO 24364:2023 — Royal jelly production** is published.
- Royal-jelly quality is multicomponent. 10-HDA is not presented as a complete freshness, authenticity or safety marker.
- Raw hive venom is kept visually and textually separate from standardised medicinal venom preparations.
- EAACI guidance supports venom immunotherapy as specialist evidence-based treatment for appropriately diagnosed Hymenoptera venom allergy.
- Deliberate self-stinging, raw-venom injection and unregulated self-desensitisation are explicitly rejected as substitutes for VIT.
- The 2026 analytical-standardisation literature reinforces that melittin alone is not a complete quality proxy; multicomponent evidence is needed.
- No dedicated published bee-venom product standard was identified in the ISO bee-product listings checked on 18 September 2026.

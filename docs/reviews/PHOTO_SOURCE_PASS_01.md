## 1. A_CORE sourcing classes

The 40 A_CORE photographs are now separated by evidence requirement:

- **16 EVIDENCE_AUTHENTIC_ONLY** — biological/diagnostic/documentary states that must be real, provenance-controlled evidence;
- **16 FIELD_PROCESS_AUTHENTIC** — real field or procedure photography;
- **8 CONTROLLED_REAL_CAPTURE** — real controlled still-life/equipment/product capture.

A generated image is never accepted under a `Photo x.y` identifier. If a generated visual is useful, it belongs to the illustration/diagram system and must be disclosed/managed there instead.

## 2. Rights and technical suitability are separate gates

The source registry tracks:

1. whether the image really depicts the required subject;
2. whether diagnosis/provenance is strong enough;
3. whether commercial print/digital reuse is permitted;
4. whether resolution is adequate for the intended placement;
5. whether attribution or other conditions apply.

No asset becomes final merely because it is easy to download.

## 3. First authoritative source family

USDA Agricultural Research Service Image Gallery was selected as the first source family because its published policy states that gallery photographs are free of charge and copyright-free/public-domain unless otherwise indicated, while requesting ARS credit and prohibiting implied endorsement.

Policy:
- https://www.ars.usda.gov/oc/images/copyright/

## Accuracy and reuse controls

- Controlled crystallisation is shown as many fine crystals versus fewer coarse crystals; no fixed seed percentage or universal temperature is embedded.
- Fermented or contaminated honey is not shown as suitable for blending back into ordinary table honey.
- Ingredient-added products trigger extra traceability/allergen/stability/labelling controls and are not treated automatically as plain honey.
- Figures 54.1 and 55.2 use one identical `WAX_SOURCE_STREAM_MASTER`.
- Figures 54.2 and 55.3 use one identical `COMB_AGE_CUTAWAY_MASTER`; old brood comb is not redrawn differently between chapters.
- Worker wax anatomy locates four paired wax-mirror regions on abdominal sternites IV–VII and shows external scale handling without pretending the schematic is to scale.
- Pollen trapping has no universal percentage or fixed trap-on schedule.
- Bee pollen and bee bread are shown as related but non-identical biological/product states without claiming a single universal fermentation recipe.
- Propolis quality begins with controlled collection; colour alone is not used as proof of identity or grade.

## Scientific / editorial controls

- Supplemental feeding is purpose-specific and temporary, not a substitute for forage, queenright status or disease diagnosis.
- Winter feeding avoids universal hive-weight, temperature and fixed-calendar thresholds.
- Condensation artwork does not prescribe one ventilation configuration for all climates.
- Queen-cell position is presented as context rather than definitive diagnosis.
- Queen failure is presented as a differential problem; brood appearance alone is not used to justify requeening.
- Splitting and queen-rearing plates include disease/Varroa-transfer and donor-colony viability cautions.
- No treatment dose, off-label chemical instruction or jurisdiction-specific legal claim is introduced.

## Controls
- food-safety graphics distinguish product-specific perishability and controls rather than treating all hive products like mature honey;
- legal diagrams are jurisdiction-neutral and explicitly require current competent-authority verification;
- traceability graphics preserve backward, forward and internal lot relationships;
- digital traceability includes backup, tested restore, access control and offline recall capability;
- insurance is shown as one risk-treatment option, not a substitute for prevention or legal compliance;
- no fixed premium, policy limit or universal insurance requirement is embedded in the artwork.

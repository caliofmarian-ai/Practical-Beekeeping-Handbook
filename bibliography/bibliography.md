# Reference 76 — Bibliography

This consolidated bibliography brings together the principal books, peer-reviewed research, standards, official guidance, and institutional resources underpinning the *Practical Beekeeping Handbook*. Chapter-level reference lists remain the most detailed source trail for specialised claims; this section provides the book-wide core bibliography and removes unnecessary duplication.

Citation style: author/organisation, title, publication/source, year, and DOI or stable official source where useful. Legal and regulatory sources are dated because requirements can change.

## Bibliographic Maintenance Rule

Before release of a new handbook edition:

1. re-check all legal and regulatory sources;
2. re-check URLs for institutional resources;
3. verify that cited 2025–2026 “current” documents remain current;
4. retain DOI-based peer-reviewed citations even if journal landing pages move;
5. remove duplicate references caused by chapter mergers;
6. never delete a foundational source merely because it is old if it remains scientifically or historically necessary;
7. never rely on an old source for current medicine authorisation, disease-reporting law, food labelling, or treatment thresholds.

*This consolidated bibliography should be proofed against the chapter-level references during the final technical review to capture any source added during late corrections.*

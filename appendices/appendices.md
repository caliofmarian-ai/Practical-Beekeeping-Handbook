# Reference 80 — Appendices

These appendices are practical working tools designed to accompany Chapters 1–74. They are intentionally jurisdiction-neutral unless stated otherwise. Legal record-retention periods, medicine records, organic-certification forms, tax documents, food-business records, and official disease forms must be adapted to the requirements of the competent authority or certification body.

# Appendix R — Traceability Lot-Code Template

One practical structure:

`APIARY-HARVESTDATE-PROCESSBATCH-PACKRUN`

Example:

`DUB03-20260822-E04-P02`

Meaning:

- `DUB03` — apiary identifier;
- `20260822` — harvest date;
- `E04` — fourth extraction/processing batch;
- `P02` — second packing run.

The exact format is optional. The required outcome is unique, durable, and reconstructable traceability.

## Lot-code rules

- never reuse a code for a different product;
- preserve source-lot links after blending;
- record rework explicitly;
- keep customer/distribution links where required;
- do not encode information so cryptically that only one person can interpret it.

## Breakage response

1. Stop production.
2. Segregate exposed food and packaging.
3. Remove visible fragments using controlled tools.
4. Clean the defined contamination zone.
5. inspect and document.
6. discard affected food where safety cannot be demonstrated.
7. release only after verification.

# Appendix Z — Unit-Cost Worksheet

## Annual cost categories

- colony replacement / queens;
- feed;
- medicines/monitoring;
- frames/foundation;
- packaging;
- labels;
- fuel/vehicle;
- market fees;
- insurance;
- certification;
- laboratory tests;
- utilities;
- depreciation;
- repairs;
- labour, including owner labour;
- losses/waste;
- finance/interest where relevant.

## Calculation

`full cost per saleable unit = total attributable annual cost ÷ saleable units`

Track separately by product when possible: extracted honey, comb honey, wax, pollen, queens, nucs, pollination, training, or other services.

# Appendix AF — Annual Operation Review

At the end of each season, review:

### Colony performance
- winter survival;
- queen replacement;
- Varroa trends;
- swarming;
- disease events;
- productivity;
- feed use.

### Product performance
- harvest mass;
- moisture;
- extraction loss;
- product returns;
- fermentation;
- authenticity/origin issues;
- lot traceability.

### Business performance
- revenue by product/channel;
- full cost per unit;
- labour hours;
- capital utilisation;
- insurance claims/incidents;
- customer retention;
- unsold stock.

### Sustainability
- forage gaps;
- apiary density;
- habitat actions;
- pesticide incidents;
- fuel/transport use;
- waste and wax recycling.

### Next-year actions
1. keep what worked;
2. stop or redesign what failed;
3. define three measurable improvements;
4. assign dates and responsible people;
5. update the annual monitoring and purchasing plan.

## Appendix Use Rule

Forms are only useful when they change decisions. Do not collect data because a template exists. Define who records the information, when it is reviewed, what triggers action, how long it must be retained, and whether a legal or certification body requires a different format.
